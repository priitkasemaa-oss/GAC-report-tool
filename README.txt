GAC BID COMPASS

1. Keep index.html, standalone.js and standalone.css in the same folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open the tool in a browser.
3. Upload the Google Ads Campaign report CSV.
4. Upload the Lightdash attributed-conversions CSV.
5. Paste the current GOOGLEFINANCE USDGBP rate if it differs from the workbook fallback of 0.738475, and review the 15-month payback assumption.
6. Use or export the recommended Google Ads targets.

NorthAm targets are shown in USD. Every other campaign target is shown in GBP.
The tool processes CSV files locally in the browser and does not upload campaign data.
